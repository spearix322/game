HOW TO PLAY

⌨ CONTROLS

WASD / Arrow Keys — Move hero

Space / Z — Attack

X / Shift — Use skill

E — Interact / Pick up

I — Inventory

K — Skill tree

M — Map

Esc — Pause / Back

⚡ GAMEPLAY

Explore the open world across 4 zones

Kill enemies to gain XP and level up

Collect weapons, armor and medicine

Upgrade stats and unlock skills

Find the boss of each zone to progress

Use healing items when HP is low

📊 STATS

STR — Melee damage

AGI — Speed & dodge

INT — Skill power

VIT — Max HP

Each level → +3 stat points
